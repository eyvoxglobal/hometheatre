About Eyvox Global

Eyvox Global is a team of well-skilled and experienced professionals, with 10+ members specializing in different areas of theatre installation.

With more than 12 years of experience in the home theatre field, we create professionally designed and engineered entertainment spaces. We also undertake Education Theatre projects for schools and colleges, as well as rooftop theatre projects.

Our experience extends beyond India, with projects and professional work carried out in the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and Saudi Arabia.
